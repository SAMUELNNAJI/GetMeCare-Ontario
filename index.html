Why GetMeCare

Safety and Transparency Built Into
Every Step

We built GetMeCare for Ontario families who want the quality of a private caregiver — without the opacity of a traditional agency. Every safeguard is visible, every action is logged.

Ontario VSC Verified

Every caregiver must present a Vulnerable Sector Check issued within 12 months. We inspect the original — no exceptions.

Admin-Mediated Matching

No direct contact between families and caregivers until our admin team has reviewed schedule compatibility and confirmed terms.

Canadian Data Residency

All personal data, health logs, ID uploads, and payment tokens are stored exclusively in Canada Central (Montréal) servers — fully PIPEDA-aligned.

Live Shift Tracking

Clock-in and clock-out timestamps are recorded the moment a caregiver taps their dashboard. Families see every log in real time.

No Charge Until Shift Completes

Your card is never charged automatically. Our admin manually triggers each payment only after a completed, dispute-free shift is confirmed.

Admin Chat — Always On

Our live chat desk is monitored by our administrative team. Every message, every reply, every note — permanently logged and retrievable.
