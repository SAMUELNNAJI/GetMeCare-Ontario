FAQ

Common Questions

How long does matching take?

Most families receive their first caregiver match within 48–72 hours of posting their request.

Can I meet the caregiver before hiring?

Yes. Once admin confirms compatibility, a brief intro call can be arranged before the first shift is locked in.

What if a caregiver cancels last minute?

Our admin team is notified immediately and will work to find a replacement or rescheduled shift as quickly as possible.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. We never store raw card data. All payments are tokenized via our PCI-DSS compliant processor and stored on Canada Central servers.

Can caregivers set their own rates?

Yes. Caregivers list their preferred hourly rate on their profile. Families can filter by budget range when browsing.

What is the $39 CAD vetting fee?

It's a one-time fee for employers that covers admin compliance review, caregiver vetting, and publishing your care request to our network. There are no recurring charges.
