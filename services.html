Why GetMeCare

Every Service Comes With Our
Full Compliance Guarantee

We don't just connect you — we verify, mediate, and support every engagement from first request to final shift.

VSC-Cleared Caregivers

Every caregiver presents a Vulnerable Sector Check issued within 12 months. We inspect originals — no exceptions.

Credential Verified

PSW certificates, RPN training, and First Aid credentials are confirmed by our admin team before any profile goes live.

Admin-Mediated Matching

No direct contact until our team has reviewed compatibility and confirmed terms. You're never left on your own.

Live Shift Tracking

Clock-in and clock-out timestamps are logged the moment a caregiver taps their dashboard. Families see every log in real time.

$0 Agency Fee

You pay your caregiver directly at their hourly rate. No agency markup, no inflated invoices. Just fair, transparent pricing.

Canadian Data Residency

All personal data is stored exclusively in Canada Central (Montréal) — fully PIPEDA-aligned with no cross-border transfers.

Ready to Find the Right Caregiver?

Post a care request in minutes and our admin team will match you with a verified caregiver within 48–72 hours. No agency fees, no guesswork.
